Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about rooftop solar installations.

The total cost depends on your required system capacity in kilowatts (kW), roof type, and electrical layout. Most residential homes choose between 2 kW and 5 kW systems. Typical systems pay for themselves within 3 to 4 years through monthly electricity bill savings.

Yes, residential rooftop solar installations are often supported by national and state renewable energy incentive programs. Eligibility criteria and subsidy amounts vary depending on system capacity, meter type, and current guidelines. We help you with all the required documentation and portal applications.

Solar panels have no moving parts and require very little maintenance. Gently washing the panel glass with clean water once or twice a month to clear dust or pine needles is usually plenty. SunVolt also offers routine checkup visits to keep your system performing at peak output.

Yes, modern photovoltaic panels produce electricity from ambient diffused daylight even on overcast or rainy days, generally delivering 20% to 40% of their rated output. During nights or severe downpours, your building automatically switches to regular grid supply or battery reserves.
